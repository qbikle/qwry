Rentals peak in the *last* week of every month, and the peak is getting flatter: July's was 31% above the month's median, August's 12%.

Two stores carry the whole seasonal swing. Store 2 rents 1.9x what store 1 does in the last week and the same as store 1 in the first, so the swing is one catalogue, not one city. The [returns policy](https://example.test/policy) changed on 2026-06-01, which is where the flattening starts.
